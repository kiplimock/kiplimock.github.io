I build computer vision tools for monitoring forests, so conservation can scale.

I am a PhD Candidate in EEE at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, based at the Centre for Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAIL) and supervised by Prof. Ciira wa Maina.

For my doctoral research, I am developing uncertainty-aware 3D reconstruction methods for forest environments, so that tree-level and stand-level measurements derived from images are reported together with estimates of their reliability. Besides digital forestry, I also research how deep learning can be applied to satellite imagery to monitor crops and predict yield.

Select Works

Monitoring Forests with Machine Learning

Miti360 dataset

A multi-modal dataset for reforestation monitoring from a 770-ha reforested stand in Kieni Forest, Kenya. It combines high resolution drone imagery with tree crown bounding boxes, single and stereo terrestrial images, tree biophysical parameters with species and GPS coordinates, and temporal weather data.

Dataset websitearXiv preprintRepository

TreeVision

Software tool that estimates tree DBH, height, and crown diameter from a pair of stereo photos. Its goal is to facilitate a non-contact approach for obtaining tree measurements without using expensive laser scanners.

CodePaper in SoftwareXDW feature

Estimating Tree Measurements using Stereoscopic Photogrammetry

A field study showing that stereo cameras and open software can replace contact measurements in a real-world forest (in Kenya), at a fraction of the usual cost.

Deep learning on drone images tracks the growth of a reforested stand.

About

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, supervised by Prof. Ciira wa Maina and based at DSAIL. My research builds AI methods for environmental conservation and agriculture. I started as a research intern on TreeVision, which became the basis of my MSc research at DeKUT.

My main interests are computer vision, especially 3D perception of the environment, photogrammetry and uncertainty-aware reconstruction, and probabilistic machine learning, including decision theory, uncertainty quantification and recommender systems.

Away from work I read, watch documentaries and hike. Ask me about my favourite trails.
